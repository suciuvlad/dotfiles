# Marketing Brain Launch Video Script

Target runtime: about 3:45 at 145-155 words per minute.

## Voiceover

Most SEO work starts too late.

People open a keyword tool, pull a list, export a spreadsheet, and then try to
turn that mess into a strategy.

The problem is not that the data is missing. The problem is that the data never
becomes a working brain.

It sits in tabs. It sits in exports. It sits in reports that nobody wants to
open again.

Marketing Brain is built for the step after research.

It takes competitor and keyword data from DataForSEO, turns it into a structured
Obsidian strategy vault, and then synthesizes the ULTIMATE BEAST plan: a
practical execution map for AI search, AI Overviews, and Google SERP.

No black-hat tricks. No fake guarantees. No fluffy strategy doc that sounds
good once and dies in a folder.

The product ships as two artifacts.

First, a pre-built Obsidian vault. It follows the Hot, Index, Wiki pattern:
hot context for what matters now, an index for navigation, and deep notes for
everything that should not live in working memory.

Second, the Marketing Brain skill. This is the automation layer that runs the
six-step research pipeline and fills the vault with real evidence.

The pipeline starts by finding the top competitors for the site.

Then it pulls the keywords each competitor ranks for.

Then it builds a deduplicated workbook, sorted into high opportunity, hidden
gems, high volume, and all keywords.

Then it mines People Also Ask and related SERP questions, because search intent
does not live in keyword volume alone.

Then it scaffolds the Obsidian vault, applies the right business-type strategy,
and writes the source notes.

Finally, it synthesizes the BEAST plan: FLOW, competitor data, keyword data,
page opportunities, risks, and next actions in one place.

The key is not the spreadsheet. The key is continuity.

When you come back tomorrow, the brain still knows where you are.

The hot note tells you what changed, what is blocking, and what the next move
should be.

The index keeps the vault navigable. The wiki keeps the depth available without
turning every session into a context dump.

That matters because marketing strategy is not one report. It is a loop.

Read first. Write second. Verify third.

The system also includes guardrails where they matter.

Credentials stay in environment variables. DataForSEO spend has caps. Generated
vaults keep raw source material separated from client-facing deliverables.

Visual reference capture is built in too, so when you need screenshots,
downloaded page images, or project images, the vault can store them as evidence
and style references instead of mixing them into production assets without a
license trail.

What you get back is not just an answer.

You get a working strategy vault, a keyword workbook, source notes, visual
references, and a client-facing plan that can keep evolving.

That is why this is proprietary.

Marketing Brain is for people who bought it, and for AI Marketing Hub Pro
members. It is source-available for operators who want to run the system, adapt
it for their clients, and keep the strategic brain alive after the first report.

If your SEO process ends at an export, this will feel different.

The export becomes a map.

The map becomes a vault.

The vault becomes the place where the next action is always easier to find.

That is Marketing Brain.

Strategic SEO, orchestrated by artificial intelligence.

Read first. Write second. Verify third.

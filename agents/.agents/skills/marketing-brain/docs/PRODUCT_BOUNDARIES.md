# Product Boundaries

Marketing Brain is a read-only strategic operating system for SEO and content
marketing work. It turns exports, source notes, and optional DataForSEO pulls
into an Obsidian vault, keyword workbook, BEAST plan, and client-ready report.

## What It Does

- Scaffolds a reusable Obsidian vault for one brand or client.
- Preserves raw source exports under `.raw/` and tracks them in manifests.
- Enriches research with DataForSEO competitor, keyword, and SERP/PAA data.
- Produces a source-cited strategy plan, scorecards, next actions, and PDF/HTML reports.
- Supports Codex, Claude, and shell-based operators.

## What It Does Not Do

- It does not mutate Google Search Console, GA4, CMS, DNS, GBP, or ad accounts.
- It does not guarantee rankings, traffic, AI Overview inclusion, or revenue.
- It does not publish pages, create backlinks, buy links, or request reviews.
- It does not store API keys, OAuth tokens, CMS credentials, or passwords.
- It does not replace human verification before a page prune, redirect, migration, or publication decision.

## Required Trust Gates

No optimization recommendation should be treated as approved until the relevant
source, confidence, owner, approval status, and rollback note are present.
For SEO/AIO work, the Day 0 measurement gate must close before forecasts,
traffic-loss diagnosis, or recovery promises are presented to a client.
